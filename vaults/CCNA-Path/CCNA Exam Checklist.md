# CCNA Exam Checklist

> [!abstract] Why this matters
> This is every topic on the CCNA 200-301 exam, sorted into the exam's six parts, with the note that teaches it. Tick a box when you can explain the topic out loud and do its lab without help. The boxes you haven't ticked are what to study next.

> [!info] About the exam
> The exam is called **200-301 CCNA**. It lasts 120 minutes. It has multiple-choice questions, drag-and-drop questions, and lab questions where you type into a simulated device. You can't go back to a question once you've answered it. Book it through Pearson VUE, at a test centre or online. Check Cisco's website for the current topic list before you book, because it changes from time to time.

## The last two weeks

1. Do every gate in [[CCNA Phase Gates]] again, without looking.
2. Rebuild two capstones from [[Labs/Capstone Labs]] from a blank workspace.
3. Do 20 subnetting drills a day from [[Cheat Sheet - Subnetting]], against a timer.
4. Revise the cheat-sheet flashcards every day. Clear the ones you keep getting wrong.
5. Sit at least one full practice exam under real timing.
6. The day before, stop at lunchtime. Rest is part of the plan.
